# Bratfein Webseite

Create a modern, premium landing page and multi-page web application for "Bratfein", a Swiss YES-startup selling high-quality local Beef Tallow (Rindertalg) in 250g jars (priced at 8-10 CHF).

1. BRANDING & VISUAL DESIGN:

- Aesthetics: High-end gourmet combined with traditional craftsmanship.

- Color Palette: Clean white background with generous whitespace, dark charcoal text, warm copper/gold accents, and subtle dark wood texture/grain elements for cards/headers.

- Typography: Premium serif for main headers, clean modern sans-serif for body text.

- Language: Swiss German (Schweizer Hochdeutsch). Always use "ss" instead of "ß" (e.g., "gross", "Genuss", "Schliessen").

- Placeholders: Use elegant visual placeholders for product images and team photos.

2. NAVIGATION & STRUCTURE:

Create a sticky header with smooth navigation between the following pages/sections:

- Home (Startseite)

- Über uns (YES-Projekt)

- Referenzen & Standorte (Märkte)

- Shop (Bestellung)

3. DETAILED PAGE CONTENT:

A) HOME PAGE:

- Hero Section: Large compelling title ("Der natürliche Genuss fürs perfekte Steak"), high-end product placeholder, and two CTA buttons: "Jetzt bestellen" & "Märkte entdecken".

- Feature Grid: Highlight high smoke point (hoher Rauchpunkt), 100% regional sourcing from a local Zurich butcher, Nose-to-Tail sustainability.

- "Warum Rindertalg statt Samenöle?" Section:

  * Engaging/dramatic storytelling about the heavy processing of modern seed oils.

  * A clear visual comparison graphic/card comparing Beef Tallow vs. Industrial Seed Oils (Smoke Point, Processing, Flavor, Naturalness).

B) ÜBER UNS (TEAM):

- Tell the story of 7 passionate Swiss high school students operating within the YES (Young Enterprise Switzerland) program.

- Introduce team roles with placeholder cards: Tim (CEO), Finn (CAO), Nils & Melissa (CMO), Stefan (CFO), Valerio (CTO), Victor (CPO).

- Emphasize local butcher partnership in Zurich.

C) REFERENZEN & STANDORTE:

- References/Testimonials section (quotes from steak enthusiasts and market customers).

- Upcoming Markets Section: Modern interactive cards showing upcoming Christmas and community market locations (Place, Date, Time, Location Badge).

D) SHOP / BESTELLFORMULAR:

- Simple, elegant product showcase of the neutral 250g jar (8-10 CHF).

- Lightweight Order Form (no complex payment gateway needed):

  * Input fields: Name, Email, Address, Quantity (250g jars), Pickup at market OR Delivery preference, Notes.

  * Payment Note: "Zahlung bequem via Twint oder Bar vor Ort / nach Absprache".

  * Submit button: "Bestellung anfragen".

Make the entire site fully responsive, elegant, smooth-scrolling, and mobile-friendly.
